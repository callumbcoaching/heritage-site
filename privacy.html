Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

This policy explains how CALLUM.B ("I", "me", "my") collects and uses your personal data when you use this website or my coaching services. I'm the data controller for the information you share with me.

What I collect

  • Contact & enquiry details — name, email, phone and anything you write when you submit an application or download a free guide.
  • Coaching data — if you become a client: stats, goals, training/nutrition info and progress you share with me.
  • Payment data — handled securely by my payment provider (Stripe). I don't store your card details.
  • Usage data — basic, privacy-friendly website analytics (pages visited) to improve the site.

How I use it & legal basis

To respond to your enquiry, deliver guides and coaching, take payment, and (with your consent) send occasional coaching emails. Legal bases: consent (marketing emails, which you can withdraw anytime), contract (delivering coaching you've paid for), and legitimate interests (running and improving my business).

Who I share it with

Only trusted providers that help run the service: website hosting, form handling (Formspree), payments (Stripe) and the coaching app (1FIT), email marketing (e.g. MailerLite) and website analytics. Each processes your data under their own terms. I never sell your data.

How long I keep it

Enquiry data is kept while I follow up and for a reasonable period after; client records are kept for the duration of coaching and as required for tax/legal reasons; you can ask me to delete your data at any time.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any right, just contact me. You also have the right to complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

This site uses essential cookies and, only with your consent, analytics (Google Analytics) and advertising (Meta/Facebook Pixel) cookies — these help me understand site traffic and show relevant ads. On your first visit you'll see a banner where you can Accept or Decline; declining means no analytics or advertising cookies are set. You can clear your browser cookies any time to be asked again.
